Autocomplete○ medium · added

Predictions grouped by parts and categories, with SKUs. For a part-number catalog this is the highest-leverage add — people half-remember a SKU and the dropdown closes the gap. Results are pre-filtered to the garage vehicle ("24 fit your F-150").

No-results path○ medium

An empty query never dead-ends. Zero matches shows the universal SKUs plus "widen to all trucks" — the rule is no blank "0 results" screen, especially behind a fitment filter.
